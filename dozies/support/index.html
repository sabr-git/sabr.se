Dozies support

Questions or found a bug? Email support@sabr.se. Please tell us what happened and which iPhone and iOS version you use. We answer in English and Swedish.

The alarm doesn't ring

Open Settings › Dozies and check that Alarms are allowed. Keep the app installed; alarms ring even when the app is closed.

Send us a log

If an alarm didn't behave as expected, you can export the alarm log in the app and email it to us. The log contains no health data.

Restore purchases

Bought Dozies Plus on another iPhone? Open the Plus page in the app and tap Restore Purchases.

Cancel your subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Dozies. Plus keeps working until the end of the period you have paid for.
