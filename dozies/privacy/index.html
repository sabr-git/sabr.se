Privacy Policy for Dozies

Effective November 18, 2026

In short: Dozies does not collect any personal data. The app has no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.

Controller

Dozies is developed by SABR Consulting AB (reg. no. 559414-2894), Sweden. Contact: support@sabr.se.

Stored on your phone only

  • Alarms and settings: alarm times, chosen games, sounds and snooze, used to run your alarms.
  • Sleep from Apple Health: if you allow it, Dozies reads your recorded sleep from the last 24 hours and shows it in the morning summary. Dozies never writes to Health, does not store sleep data and never sends it anywhere.
  • Motion sensor: used only while you play, to steer the game. Nothing is stored.

None of this leaves your phone, and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Purchases

Dozies Plus is purchased through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; we never receive your payment details or identity. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Troubleshooting log

The app can export a technical alarm log. It contains alarm times, app version and technical events, but no health data. It is only sent if you choose to share it, for example with us by email. We use it only to fix the problem and delete it afterwards.

Your rights

Since we hold no data about you, there is normally nothing to access or delete. If you have emailed us, you can ask us to delete your messages. You may also lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
